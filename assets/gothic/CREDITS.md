# Gothic artwork

The images in this folder were processed (cropped, converted to a tintable
white-with-alpha form, downscaled) by `tools/gothic_assets.py` from reference
images supplied by the project owner.

**Provenance:** the original artists and sources are not known. These files
are third-party material, are **not** covered by the project's LGPL-3.0-only
licence, and are included at the owner's decision. If you are an artist whose
work appears here and you would like it credited or removed, please open an
issue at https://github.com/Hanishchow/Dex and it will be handled promptly.
